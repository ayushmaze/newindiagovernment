# The New India Government — New Features (6 June 2026)

A summary of everything added to the site in today's session. The goal was to
make the site more engaging, shareable, and mobile-first, with researched and
sourced fact-check content at its core.

All changes are on `main` (commits `de9342b` → `965dbe6`) and deploy to
**https://newindiagovernment.com** via Vercel's auto-deploy on push.

---

## 1. The Jumla Meter — `/promises`

An interactive, filterable scorecard of major government promises, each weighed
against the public record.

- **7 promises tracked**, each with: an animated progress meter (% delivered
  against the headline claim), a colour-coded verdict, a one-line punchline,
  expandable evidence, and citations.
- **Verdicts:** `BROKEN`, `JUMLA`, `DELAYED`, `PARTIALLY MET`, `ON TRACK`.
- **Filter bar** (All / Broken / Jumla / Delayed / Partial) and a live
  scoreboard ("X/7 broken or jumla", "avg % delivered").
- **Per-card share** button.
- A **compact 3-card version** also appears on the homepage, linking through to
  the full meter.

**The tracked promises (all sourced):**

| Promise | Verdict | What the record shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 crore jobs every year | BROKEN | Net loss of ~70 lakh jobs 2016-17 → 2022-23 (CMIE) |
| ₹15 lakh in every account | JUMLA | Called "a jumla" on record by Amit Shah |
| Double farmers' income by 2022 | BROKEN | ICRIER found <50% of target met |
| Make in India = 25% of GDP | BROKEN | Manufacturing share fell to ~14% |
| 100 Smart Cities | PARTIALLY MET | Only 18 of 100 cities finished all projects |
| Tap water to every home by 2024 | DELAYED | ~98% taps, but ~75% reliable; deadline → 2028 |
| Clean Ganga (Namami Gange) | PARTIALLY MET | Real progress, repeatedly delayed |

Data lives in `src/lib/promises.ts` (typed, with source links), so it's easy to
edit or migrate into the CMS later.

## 2. Real or Jumla? — `/quiz`

An addictive, replayable fact-check game.

- Shows a famous claim; the player taps **REAL** or **JUMLA**.
- Instant feedback: correct/incorrect, the true verdict, a plain-English
  explanation, and a **source link**.
- **Streak counter**, running score, and a **personal best** saved locally
  (`localStorage`), so returning players have something to beat.
- **Confetti** on a correct answer; a shake animation on a wrong one.
- **Results card** at the end with a rank ("Jumla Detector 🥇", "Sharp
  Citizen 🧠", etc.) and a **"Challenge a friend"** share button.
- Questions are **shuffled** each play. A teaser section on the homepage drives
  players in.

Questions live in `src/lib/quizQuestions.ts` (each with explanation + source).

## 3. The Movement — `/movement`

A dedicated page presenting **The New India Government as a citizens' movement**
(not a party).

- Hero with the movement framing ("Not a party. Not a personality. A movement.")
- **Four principles** ("Numbers can't be hidden", "Evidence over noise",
  "No leaders to buy off", "Patience is not silence").
- **Three ways to participate**, linking to the Jumla Meter, the quiz, and
  petitions.
- A **live, ticking membership counter** with a "Join the movement" button
  (remembers if you've joined, via `localStorage`) and share buttons.
- A decorative animated crowd of **marching citizens** (raised-fist mark)
  drifting across the dark banner — purely CSS-driven and disabled under
  reduced-motion.

The same live banner also appears on the homepage.

> Note: this section started as a "Swarm" concept and was rebranded to The New
> India Government per your direction; all earlier naming was removed.

## 4. One-tap sharing (virality)

A reusable **ShareButton** used across the new features.

- Uses the **native share sheet** on mobile (where sharing actually happens).
- Falls back to **copy-to-clipboard** on desktop, with a "Copied!"
  confirmation.
- Each share comes pre-written with a punchy, on-message caption.

File: `src/components/share/ShareButton.tsx`.

---

## 5. Animations

A small, performant animation system — all of it honours
`prefers-reduced-motion`.

- **`<Reveal>`** — a reusable scroll-reveal wrapper (fade/slide/scale/blur
  variants, with stagger delays), built on a single IntersectionObserver.
  Used throughout the new pages.
- **New CSS keyframes** added to `globals.css`: scroll reveal, marching
  "scuttle", subtle wiggle, pop-in, shake, confetti fall, gradient "sheen" on
  CTAs, gentle float, count-flip, and a tap-shrink press effect for touch.
- Animated progress meters and the live counters tie it together.

## 6. Mobile experience

- **App-style bottom tab bar** (mobile only): Home · Jumla · Quiz · Join · Vote,
  with active states and safe-area padding for notched phones.
  File: `src/components/nav/MobileTabBar.tsx`.
- **Tap feedback** (`tap-shrink`) on interactive elements.
- All new sections are built responsive-first (fluid type via `clamp()`,
  single-column → multi-column grids).
- Layout padding added so content never hides behind the tab bar.
